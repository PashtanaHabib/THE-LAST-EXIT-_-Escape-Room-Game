THE LAST EXIT ⚙️

Briefing

How to Play

🎯 Goal

Escape through 4 rooms by solving 12 puzzles (3 per room) before the 20-minute timer reaches zero. Information from earlier rooms is needed in the final room.

🖱️ Investigate

Click objects in the room. Some give clues, some open puzzles. Clues you find are stored in your Inventory — click an item there to read it again.

🔓 Unlock rooms

Solve all three puzzles of a room, then click the exit door. Completing a room unlocks the next one in Room Selection.

💡 Hints

Every puzzle has a hint button. A hint costs 50 points (only the first time you open it for that puzzle).
